How to join

Sign up in person

Registration is done at the front desk, where the team helps you with everything.

  1. Visit the reception

    1st Level, R.C. Intino Building 2. Bring a valid ID if you are a student or senior.

  2. Fill out the forms

    The staff help you with the forms and set up your biometrics for entry.

  3. Pick your plan

    Annual membership is ₱500. Then choose monthly or per session, whatever fits your schedule.
